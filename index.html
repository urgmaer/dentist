Our Story

Where Science Meets a Genuine Smile

Founded in 2009 by Dr. Shirin Akter, Today's Dentistry was born from a simple belief: every person deserves dental care that is both exceptional and genuinely kind. What started as a single-chair practice in Gulshan has grown into Dhaka's most trusted dental destination.

Dr. Akter completed her BDS at Dhaka Dental College and her postgraduate training in Cosmetic & Implant Dentistry at the Royal College of Surgeons, London. She leads a team of eight specialist dentists, all united by a commitment to pain-free, precision dentistry.

Our clinic is equipped with the latest CBCT imaging, digital smile design software, and laser dentistry — all within a warm, welcoming environment designed to make every visit comfortable.
